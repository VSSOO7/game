? Kaun Sus Hai?

How to Play

  1. Enter 3–12 names and choose a pack. One person secretly gets a different question.
  2. Pass the phone. Open your question in private and answer in 1–5 words.
  3. Read everyone’s answers together. Tap one to put its author in the Hot Seat.
  4. Pass the phone again and vote in private. You can’t vote for yourself.
  5. If the imposter alone gets the most votes, everyone else earns 1 point. If the imposter escapes, they earn 2 points. A tie for first counts as an escape.
