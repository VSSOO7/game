How to Play

  1. Scan the invite QR code or open the invite link to play on your phone. The host can also add players to a shared device.
  2. Each round draws one question from a random pack. Answer in 1–5 words. The imposter secretly gets a different question.
  3. Tap an answer for the Hot Seat. Keep tapping a player’s name to nudge them privately. Players on their own devices can type or record a reply.
  4. Chat with the room while voting. Vote for one other player. Once every ballot is locked, the room can see who voted for whom. The imposter escapes if there’s a tie for first or someone else gets the most votes.
  5. When the countdown ends, the group earns 1 point each if they caught the imposter. Otherwise the imposter earns 2 points.
